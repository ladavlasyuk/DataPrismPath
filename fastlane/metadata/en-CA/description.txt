Prism Path is a calm and thoughtful light puzzle where you guide a beam through a series of handcrafted chambers to illuminate every crystal. Rotate mirrors, prisms, color filters, gateways, and other elements to redirect the beam and create the correct path across each grid. Mirrors bend the light around corners, prisms split one beam into two, color filters change the beam's color so it can activate matching crystals, and gateways transport the beam across the chamber to open otherwise inaccessible routes. Barriers add additional challenges by blocking paths and forcing you to find alternative solutions. The beam updates instantly after every move, allowing you to clearly understand the effect of each action and solve every chamber through observation and careful planning. Progress through sixteen handcrafted levels with a gradual difficulty curve, complete each chamber within its target move count to earn up to three stars, and unlock new challenges as you advance. Track detailed statistics including moves, illuminated crystals, beam splits, gateway jumps, color changes, and time spent. Customize the experience with eight visual light palettes, optional sound effects, haptic feedback, and beam trail effects, while saving your progress and player portrait locally on the device. Designed for quiet, one-handed portrait play, Prism Path offers a relaxing puzzle experience without timers, lives, or pressure.
